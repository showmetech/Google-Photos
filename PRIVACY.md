# Privacy

The app reads folders explicitly selected by the user and uploads selected photos and videos to the Google Photos account authorized by that user. Uploads require Google consent. Originals are not deleted by the app. Uploads consume Google account storage under Google's terms.

Configuration, authorization tokens, file inventory and operational receipts are kept in this Windows user's application data. They are not sent to GitHub, the developer or an analytics service. The release workflow must never package configuration, tokens, databases, photos or user logs.

Revoke Google authorization in your Google account to stop future access. Local operational history can contain filenames and should be treated as private. Microsoft Store delivery may collect the platform's own installation and crash information under Microsoft's policy.

Source and support: https://github.com/showmetech/Google-Photos/issues
